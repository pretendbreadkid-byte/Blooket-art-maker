Privacy

Blook Berry is a free fan-made art maker for Blooket-style art. It is not made by or connected to Blooket. This page says what the site keeps and why.

What we keep

  • Your account: the username you choose and your password, stored scrambled (hashed) so nobody can read it.
  • Your art: projects, packs and pictures you upload, so you can open them again on any device.
  • Guests: if you play as a guest, nothing is saved on our server.

We do not ask for your real name, email, address or phone number, and we do not sell anything about you.

Your browser

The site saves a few settings in your browser (like which panel sections are open) so it remembers how you like things.

Ads

Blook Berry shows ads from Google AdSense to help pay for the site. Google may use cookies to show ads and count how many people see them. Because many players are young, the site asks Google to treat it as made for children, so ads are not personalised. You can read how Google uses data at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites.

Deleting your stuff

You can delete any project from the home page. To have your whole account removed, ask the site's admin.
