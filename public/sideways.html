How to play

Each run is two laps. Slide through corners to earn points, finish to earn cash, then spend it in the garage.

Gamepads work too: left stick, triggers, and A for the handbrake.

Steer with the arrows on the left. Gas, brake and the Drift button (a handbrake) are on the right.

Drifting

Turn in at speed with the throttle held and the rear steps out. Ease the steering to hold the angle, steer the other way to straighten up, or tap the handbrake to start a slide on purpose. Lifting off the gas brings the grip back.

Scoring

Points build while you slide: more speed and more angle score faster. Start a new drift within two seconds of the last one to raise the multiplier, up to ×9. Hit a wall or stay on the dirt and the unbanked chain is gone. Stranger vehicles and harder circuits multiply everything you earn.

Circuits

Finishing a run unlocks the next circuit. Every surface grips differently, and the volcano has lava where the runoff should be.
